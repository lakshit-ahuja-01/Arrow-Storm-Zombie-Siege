# UI & Web Design Anti-Patterns and Guidelines

Always strictly avoid the following overused, cluttered, or cliché design elements:

## 1. Visual Styles & Effects to Avoid
- **Harsh gradients & rainbow coloring**: Avoid overpowering multicolor schemes or harsh linear gradients.
- **Ubiquitous drop shadows**: Do not slap heavy drop shadows on every card, button, and container.
- **Liquid glass / Glassmorphism**: Avoid overly glossy, translucent panels that degrade text contrast and legibility.
- **Radial orbs & dot grids**: Do not use glowing radial background orbs or decorative background dot matrices.
- **Sparkles & animated arrows**: Omit ornamental sparkle icons (`✨`) and bouncing/animated directional arrows.
- **Neon colors & generic pastels**: Avoid saturated neon highlights and bland washed-out pastels.
- **Hover animations on everything**: Avoid jittery micro-animations or scale effects on non-interactive or passive elements.

## 2. Typography & Formatting Rules
- **Font restrictions**: Avoid overused combinations of Inter, Geist, and Space Grotesque, especially when paired with awkward em-dash styling.
- **Bullet styling**: Do not use checkmark bullet points (`✓` or `✔`) as list adornments.
- **Tone**: Keep typographic hierarchy clean, legible, and dignified.

## 3. Layout & Structure Anti-Patterns
- **No 3-feature card rows**: Avoid the predictable "3 feature cards side-by-side" layout.
- **No Bento grids**: Avoid forced modular bento-box card layouts.
- **No faux terminal windows**: Avoid gimmick terminal / code prompt windows for non-developer tools.
- **No rigid 3-tier pricing tables**: Avoid generic 3-column pricing tiers.
- **Mandatory elements**: Always prioritize authentic product demonstrations / interactive demos, and real legal footers (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy).
- **No skeleton loader overuse**: Avoid empty grey skeleton loading placeholders where crisp instant loading or purposeful states are better.
- **No soft corner radius / decorative stripes**: Avoid excessively rounded pill/blob corners and decorative striped borders.
- **No fake testimonials**: Use only authentic, credible presentation.

## 4. Iconography Restrictions
- **No generic icon sets / Lucid icons**: Use tailored, purposeful iconography only when strictly necessary.
- **No decorative emojis**: Do not sprinkle emojis (`🚀`, `✨`, `🔥`, `💡`) into professional headings or body copy.
- **No ornamental sparkles**: Keep all visual cues functional and grounded.
